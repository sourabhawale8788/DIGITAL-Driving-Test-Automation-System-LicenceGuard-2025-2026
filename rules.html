Understanding Road Signs

Road signs are the language of the road that guide drivers, warn them of potential hazards, and provide essential information. Understanding these signs is crucial for safe driving and passing your RTO driving test.

🛑 Red Signs

MANDATORY - Must be followed strictly. Violations lead to penalties.

⚠️ Yellow Signs

WARNING - Alert you to potential hazards ahead.

ℹ️ Blue Signs

INFORMATORY - Provide helpful information about facilities.

Traffic Light Signals

🔴 Red Light - STOP

Come to a complete halt before the stop line. Do not cross the intersection.

🟡 Yellow Light - CAUTION

Slow down and prepare to stop. Yellow means the light is about to turn red.

🟢 Green Light - GO

Proceed with caution. Check for pedestrians and vehicles before moving.

📋 Essential Driving Rules

General Safety Rules

  • Always wear seat belt while driving
  • Do not use mobile phone while driving
  • Maintain safe distance from the vehicle ahead
  • Drive within prescribed speed limits (50-60 km/hr in city)
  • Stop before the stop line at red light
  • Always overtake from the right side
  • Use indicators before turning or changing lanes

Two-Wheeler Safety

  • Wear ISI marked helmet (both rider and pillion)
  • Ensure helmet is properly fastened
  • Keep both front and rear lights functional
  • Do not carry more than one pillion rider
  • Ride in the middle lane, not in bus lane
  • Avoid zigzag riding - it's dangerous
  • Check tire pressure before riding

Right of Way Rules

  • Emergency vehicles (ambulance, fire brigade, police) have first priority
  • Pedestrians on zebra crossing have right of way
  • Give way to vehicles already in the roundabout
  • At unmanned intersections, traffic on the right has priority
  • On hilly roads, vehicles going uphill have right of way
  • Drivers turning right must give way to oncoming traffic

Pedestrian Safety

  • Cross only at zebra crossings, subways, or foot over bridges
  • Walk on footpath or facing oncoming traffic
  • Never cross from between parked vehicles
  • Use designated pedestrian crossings
  • Children below 12 years should sit in rear seat of cars

🔄 Negotiating Intersections

Did you know? Almost 50% of all city driving collisions occur at intersections.

  • Choose your lane at least 100 meters in advance
  • Left lane for left turn, middle for straight, right for right turn
  • Slow down while approaching an intersection
  • Give proper turn indicators
  • Watch for pedestrians and animals even on green signal
  • At roundabouts, merge slowly with existing traffic
  • Give way to traffic on your right

🌧️ Driving in Adverse Weather

During Rain

  • Turn on wipers and defroster
  • Drive slow and maintain extra distance
  • Be careful of water-logged areas
  • Watch for hydroplaning (tires gliding on water)
  • If hydroplaning occurs: stop acceleration, press clutch, hold steering straight

During Fog

  • Use dipped headlights, not high beams
  • Honk periodically to alert others
  • Never attempt overtaking in fog
  • Use road edge as guide
  • Drive slow but not too slow to avoid becoming a hazard

Remember: "Signs and Signals are Languages of the Road - Learn Them, Respect Them"
